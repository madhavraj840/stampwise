Terms & Privacy Policy

Early access (testing). Last updated 18 Nov 2026.

What we collect

Your mobile number and the name you type in, the shops you join, your stamps, visits and rewards. Shops can also add you with your number and name. We do not collect your location, contacts or payment details.

Why

To run your stamp cards: to show your stamps and rewards to you, and to show the shops you visit their own customers' cards so they can approve stamps and rewards. Shops can see your name, number, stamps and visits at their shop only.

Who else sees it

Nobody else. We do not sell or share your data. It is stored with our hosting and database providers only to run the service.

Your choices

Change your name any time in Profile. Delete my account (Profile) erases your name, number, cards and stamps. Shops keep only an anonymous record of rewards they already gave you. To ask anything about your data, contact the shop or us.

Terms

Stampwise is free early-access software provided as is. Rewards are offered by each shop, not by us; the shop decides whether to approve stamps and redeem rewards. Do not misuse the service (for example fake accounts or automated requests): we may block such accounts.
